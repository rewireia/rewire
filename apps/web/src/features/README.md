# features

One folder per screen. Each one owns its page component, its parts and its styles.

- `home/` — the landing page with the job title search.
- `journey/` — the frame of the journey's steps and the conversation with Rex about a job.
- `occupation/` — confirming the occupation found.
- `report/` — the exposure report of the person's week.
- `plan/` — the learning plan and the course cards.
- `timeline/` — the plan in time, on a Bryntum Gantt chart, shown at the end of the plan.
- `course/` — a course and the two ways to pay for it.
- `courses/` — the course catalogue, with Rex to search it and filters to browse it.
- `cart/` — the cart, its single PayPal Sandbox order and the enrolment that follows.
- `offers/` — the seller's discount beacons, as a learner sees them.
- `checkout/` — the PayPal button (JavaScript SDK v6), shared by the course page and the guide.
- `learn/` — studying a course up to its certificate; a listed course links to its provider.
- `certificate/` — the public, printable certificate.
- `dashboard/` — the learner's tables, with AG Grid.
- `marketplace/` — the wallet panels of the dashboard and the seller workspace of the business role.
- `guide/` — the AI guide in a side panel.
- `explorer/` — every occupation in a table or, on a wide screen, an AG Studio dashboard.
- `method/` — what Rewire is and how it works: method, limits, sources, payments, the guide and
  privacy.
- `ai-primer/` — a plain guide to generative AI.
- `library/` — public readings about AI and work, from official sources.
